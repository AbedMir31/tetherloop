# TetherLoop Positioning

## One-Liner

TetherLoop keeps long-running AI jobs online when your Mac leaves Wi-Fi.

## Primary Audience

Developers who run Claude Code, Codex, terminal agents, Cursor tasks, and long-lived local jobs on a Mac while moving between Wi-Fi and hotspot connectivity.

## Core Pain

The Mac can be kept awake, but the agent still loses internet when the developer leaves a trusted Wi-Fi network. The job may stall, fail, or require manual recovery.

## Free V1 Offer

Free and open source Mac menu bar app that:

- watches trusted Wi-Fi disconnects,
- joins one saved hotspot target,
- prevents idle sleep when enabled,
- logs locally,
- sends useful notifications,
- stores no passwords,
- sends no telemetry.

## Pro V2 Offer

Pro makes protection automatic, closed-lid capable, and data-safe:

- advanced closed-lid mode,
- process/app guard automation,
- multi-hotspot fallback,
- smart auto-return,
- hotspot data guard,
- session detection,
- preflight check,
- CLI and Shortcuts support,
- session timeline export,
- signed/notarized build and auto-updates.

## Strongest Hook

I got tired of long Claude Code/Codex runs dying when I left Wi-Fi, so I built a Mac menu bar app that switches to my saved hotspot.

## Guardrails

- Do not claim Free V1 supports closed-lid operation.
- Do not claim TetherLoop can force-enable an iPhone hotspot.
- Do not frame this as a generic Wi-Fi utility first.
- Do not hide the privacy posture.
